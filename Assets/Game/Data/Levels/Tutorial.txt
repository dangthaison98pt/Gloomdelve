name: Tutorial
sign1: The way up to Brindlemoor.  [move] moves; hold [walk] to walk slowly near an edge.  [jump] jumps: a tap for a hop, held for a high jump.
sign2: Jump at a ledge and you catch it.  [up] or [jump] pulls you up, [down] lets go.
sign3: A ladder ahead.  [up] and [down] climb it, [jump] leaps off.
sign4: [whip] swings your blade.  Landing on a creature's head stomps it - most of them.
sign5: [dash] dashes: straight through creatures and whatever they throw - try it on the snake.  A dash costs energy (the blue bar under your hearts); it comes back by itself.  One dash in the air.
sign6: [down] + [whip] picks a thing up, [whip] throws it ([up] + [whip] throws high).  Pots hold treasure - or snakes.  [enter] opens, reads, talks and enters; press it again to put a sign like this one away.
sign7: Your GEAR is BOMBS (top left).  [use] throws one, [down] + [use] sets it down.  Bombs break dirt and rubble, never the dark bedrock.  Stand back!
sign8: Hold [gear] for the GEAR WHEEL, pick the ROPE with [left] [right], let go.  Then [use] close to this wall: the rope hooks on - climb it with [up].  [inventory] opens your bag.
sign9: Falling onto SPIKES costs 2 hearts.  An arrow trap shoots at the first thing that moves in front of it - throw a rock first!  Then the chest: [enter] opens it.  Read what is in it - [inventory] opens your bag, the book is under Key items - and the gate will let you through.
